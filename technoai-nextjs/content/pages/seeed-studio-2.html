NVIDIA Jetson Thor represents a major advancement in edge AI computing, providing the processing power required for modern robotics and physical AI applications. Designed around the NVIDIA Blackwell architecture, it enables developers and organizations to deploy advanced AI models directly on autonomous systems without compromising performance.

Whether developing humanoid robots, autonomous mobile robots, AI powered inspection systems, industrial automation, or intelligent vision applications, Jetson Thor provides a scalable platform that combines high performance computing with efficient power consumption for demanding edge environments.

NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor

NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor is the flagship module within the Jetson Thor family, delivering up to 2070 FP4 TFLOPS of AI performance, 128 GB LPDDR5X memory, and a Blackwell GPU architecture in a compact embedded platform. Designed for physical AI, robotics, and next generation autonomous systems, it offers up to 7.5× the AI performance and 3.5× the energy efficiency of the previous AGX Orin generation.
