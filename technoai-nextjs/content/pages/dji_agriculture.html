SOLUTIONS FOR MODERN AGRICULTURE

Support every stage of the growing cycle with intelligent drone solutions designed to improve operational efficiency, maximise crop health, and optimise agricultural productivity across farms of all sizes.

Precision Crop Spraying

Apply pesticides, herbicides, and fungicides with high accuracy, reducing chemical waste while ensuring consistent crop coverage.

Fertiliser Distribution

Efficiently distribute granular fertilisers across large agricultural areas with uniform application and reduced manual effort.

Crop Health Monitoring

Monitor crop conditions using high resolution aerial imagery to identify stress, disease, irrigation issues, and nutrient deficiencies before they impact yields.

Field Mapping & Surveying

Generate detailed aerial maps that support field planning, crop management, drainage analysis, and precision farming strategies.

BUILT FOR MODERN AGRICULTURE

Advanced Capabilities for Precision Farming

Modern agriculture requires more than efficient equipment. It demands intelligent technologies that improve productivity, optimise resource utilisation, and support informed decision making. Our agricultural drone solutions combine autonomous flight, precision application systems, and intelligent data collection to help farmers maximise yields while reducing operational costs and environmental impact.

Advanced spraying systems, intelligent route planning, terrain following, and automated flight operations enable consistent application of crop protection products and fertilisers with minimal waste. This improves coverage accuracy while reducing labour requirements and increasing operational efficiency across diverse farming environments.

FEATURED PLATFORM

DJI Agras T100

The DJI Agras T100 is one of the flagship platforms within our agricultural drone portfolio, combining intelligent flight automation, precision spraying, high capacity payloads, and advanced terrain following capabilities into a powerful solution for modern farming. Designed to support precision agriculture, crop protection, fertiliser application, and large scale farming operations, it helps agricultural professionals improve efficiency, maximise productivity, and reduce operational costs.
