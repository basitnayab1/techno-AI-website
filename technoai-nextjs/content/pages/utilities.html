BUILT FOR INFRASTRUCTURE INSPECTION

Advanced Capabilities for Asset Management

Modern utility operations require accurate, timely information to maintain critical infrastructure safely and efficiently. Our enterprise drone solutions combine intelligent imaging, precision positioning, and automated flight capabilities to help organisations inspect assets faster, improve maintenance planning, and minimise operational disruption.

High resolution imaging, thermal sensing, laser range finding, and RTK positioning enable detailed inspections of infrastructure assets while capturing accurate data for maintenance planning, engineering analysis, and regulatory compliance.

The DJI Matrice 350 RTK is one of the flagship platforms within our utility drone portfolio, delivering exceptional reliability, RTK precision, and compatibility with advanced payloads for infrastructure inspection.
