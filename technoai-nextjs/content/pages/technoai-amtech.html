TechnoAi and AMTech Announce Strategic Partnership to Advance Industrial Automation and Digital Transformation

  • Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates
  • March 18, 2026

TechnoAi, a UAE based technology integrator specializing in AI systems, robotics, and advanced industrial solutions, has officially announced a strategic partnership with AMTech, a leading provider of industrial automation, test & measurement systems, and digital transformation technologies.

This collaboration marks a significant step toward strengthening industrial capabilities across the Middle East and Africa by delivering high performance, data driven, and intelligent engineering solutions to key sectors including manufacturing, oil & gas, energy, and infrastructure.

Expanding Industrial Intelligence and Smart Manufacturing

Through this partnership, TechnoAi will introduce AMTech’s advanced portfolio of industrial systems, enabling organizations to transition toward smarter, more efficient, and digitally connected operations.

From precision test & measurement systems to integrated IIoT platforms, automation solutions, and smart manufacturing technologies, AMTech’s solutions are designed to enhance operational visibility, improve quality, and optimize overall performance.

These systems empower industries to move beyond traditional processes by leveraging real time data, intelligent monitoring, and automated control to achieve measurable improvements in productivity and reliability.

Delivering End to End Industrial Solutions

Together, TechnoAi and AMTech will provide complete, end to end solutions covering:

  • Industrial Digital Transformation & IIoT Integration

  • Advanced Test & Measurement Systems

  • Automation & Control Systems

  • Smart Manufacturing and AI Assisted Operations

This partnership ensures that clients benefit not only from world class technologies but also from TechnoAi’s expertise in procurement, integration, deployment, and ongoing support.

Driving Efficiency, Precision, and Scalability

AMTech’s engineering driven approach aligns perfectly with TechnoAi’s mission to deliver future ready solutions. By combining precision measurement, intelligent automation, and data analytics, businesses can achieve:

  • Higher operational efficiency and reduced downtime

  • Improved product quality and compliance

  • Real time decision making through actionable insights

  • Scalable systems designed for long term growth

Strengthening TechnoAi’s Global Technology Ecosystem

This partnership further reinforces TechnoAi’s growing network of global technology partners, enabling the company to deliver a comprehensive ecosystem of advanced solutions across AI, robotics, automation, and industrial systems.

By integrating AMTech’s capabilities into its portfolio, TechnoAi continues to position itself as a trusted gateway for cutting edge technologies in the region.

Looking Ahead

As industries rapidly evolve toward automation and digitalization, the collaboration between TechnoAi and AMTech will play a key role in enabling organizations to adopt smarter, more efficient, and future ready systems.

Together, both companies are committed to driving innovation, enhancing industrial performance, and supporting the transformation of businesses across the region.
